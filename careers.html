Returns & Exchange Policy

At Parts For Less, we stand behind the quality of our auto parts. Please review our policy carefully, as it outlines strict conditions regarding exchanges, warranties, and final sales.

Sales, Returns & Exchanges Overview

While most purchases are final, Parts For Less does offer exchanges under specific circumstances. Installation is permitted solely for the purpose of verifying that the part is functional and correct. Any use beyond testing (continued driving, damage, or modification) may void eligibility.

Exchanges for Defective or Incorrect Parts

Exchanges are permitted within 7 days of purchase under the following conditions:

1. Defective Part
An exchange may be approved if:

  • The part is defective and not functioning properly
  • The defect is identified during initial installation or testing only
  • The request is made within 7 days of purchase

2. Incorrect Part Ordered
If a customer orders the wrong part, Parts For Less may offer an exchange provided that (we may ask for video proof or photos):

  • The part was installed only to verify fitment or functionality
  • The part shows no signs of damage, misuse, or modification
  • The exchange request is made within 7 days of purchase

No refunds are issued. Exchanges are subject to inspection and approval. No exchanges will be accepted after 7 days.

Non-Exchangeable Items

The following items are not eligible for exchange under any circumstances:

  • Parts that have been damaged beyond installation testing
  • Electrical components (stereo, radio, amplifier, control modules/computers, fuse box, camera/sensors)
  • Customized or special-order parts
  • Parts damaged due to improper installation, misuse, or continued operation after a defect is identified

Parts Warranty (Powertrain Only)

Parts For Less provides a 90-day parts-only warranty unless otherwise stated strictly limited to the following powertrain components:

  • Engines
  • Transmissions
  • Transfer cases
  • Differentials

This warranty covers the replacement of the defective part only, where the defect is determined to have existed at the time of sale and is attributable to Parts For Less. All other parts are sold with no warranty expressed or implied. Warranty claims are subject to inspection and approval by Parts For Less. This warranty does not cover labor costs, installation, diagnostics, towing, fluids, seals, gaskets, downtime, loss of use, or any related or consequential expenses.

Exchange Process

To request an exchange for a defective item:

  • Contact our Customer Service team within 7 days of purchase.
  • Provide proof of purchase and details of the defect.
  • If approved, you will receive instructions for returning the item.

Items returned without prior approval will not be accepted.

Shipping Costs & Damage Disclaimer

Customers are responsible for all shipping costs unless the defect is confirmed to be the result of an error on our end. Parts For Less is not responsible for any damage incurred during shipping. All parts are carefully packaged prior to shipment; however, once the shipment is in the carrier’s possession, liability transfers to the carrier.

Shipping damage will not be covered unless additional shipping insurance is purchased at the time of checkout. Without added insurance, the customer assumes all risk of loss or damage during transit. Any shipping damage must be reported directly to the carrier in accordance with their claims process.

Limited 90-Day Parts-Only Warranty (Powertrain Components)

Parts For Less provides a limited 90-day parts-only warranty unless otherwise stated exclusively for the following powertrain components: Engines, Transmissions, Transfer cases, and Differentials. This warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship that are determined to have existed at the time of sale, under normal use and service, for 90 days from the date of purchase.

Coverage Exclusions: This limited warranty does not cover:

  • Damage resulting from accidents, misuse, abuse, neglect, overheating, lack of maintenance, or improper installation
  • Any modifications, disassembly, or repairs performed without prior written authorization from Parts For Less
  • Parts used for racing, competition, off-road abuse, or commercial overloading
  • Normal wear and tear or cosmetic damage
  • Seals, gaskets, sensors, belts, hoses, fluids, batteries, filters, or routine maintenance items
  • Labor, installation, diagnostics, towing, storage, downtime, or loss of use

All non-powertrain parts are sold with no warranty expressed or implied.
